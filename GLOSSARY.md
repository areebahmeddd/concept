# Glossary

One meaning per term, used the same way everywhere. If a document uses a term differently, the document is wrong.

> **Version:** v0.1 · **Updated:** 10-10-2026 · **Owner:** Design lead
>
> **Kinds:** *In-world* words are used by characters. *Design* words are used only by the team. Names come from [D-014](DECISIONS.md#d-014).

| Term | Kind | Meaning | Defined in |

## 1. Shape of the decade

A ten-year story needs a few fixed points and room to move. We fix only what must be fixed.

| Fixed (canon, never moves) | Flexible (shaped by players and production) |
| --- | --- |
| The Conjunction, ten real years after launch | The order of Maris, Kestra, Sahr and Vantor |
| Ten Long Nights, one on each launch anniversary | How long each Convergence takes |
| Orun first, Ysolde last of the six, the False Celestial last of all | Side stories, faction outcomes and Stewardships |
| The Dawnkey rule: seven Oaths, freely given | Zero's fate ([D-017](../../DECISIONS.md#d-017)) |
| Two possible endings | Which ending happens |

Real dates, release cadence and production buffers belong to the [Ten-Year Live Plan](../11-production/ten-year-live-plan.md).

## 2. The Ages

Each Age runs from one Long Night to another. Ages II to V take their names from the Celestial freed in them. The table uses the reference order (Maris, Kestra, Sahr, Vantor) that production plans against.

| Age | Real time | Convergence | World changes | Story question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Arrival** | Launch to Year 1 | Discover and Prepare for Orun | Lumen by Skyferry | *What is happening, and who are we?* |
| **The Age of Roots** | Year 1 to Year 2 | **I · Orun**, unsealed at the first Long Night | The Spine's law softens | *Can the six be saved?* |
| **The Age of Tides** | Year 2 to Year 3 | First community choice (reference: Maris) | Depends on the Celestial | *Is force the only way?* |
| **The Age of Storms** | Year 3 to Year 4 | Second community choice (reference: Kestra) | Personal ships licensed at the third Long Night | *Who will follow?* |
| **The Age of Embers** | Year 4 to Year 6 | Third community choice (reference: Sahr) | Veil opens at the fifth Long Night | *What does saving cost?* |
| **The Age of Engines** | Year 6 to Year 7 | Fourth community choice (reference: Vantor) | Colossal Frames and wider Frame ownership | *Who are our creations for?* |
| **The Age of Hours** | Year 7 to Year 8 | **VI · Ysolde** | Zero's name revealed | *What must we remember?* |
| **The Hollow Age** | Year 8 to Year 10 | **VII · the False Celestial**, then the Dawn Rite | Hollow opens at the eighth Long Night | *Is a world that will burn again worth saving?* |

**Community choice.** From the Age of Tides, two of the remaining Domains are ready at once, and whichever meets its conditions first after its earliest date unseals first ([Celestial Convergence](../05-systems/celestial-convergence.md)). Each Celestial's story beats and story question travel with that Celestial to whichever Age it lands in. World unlocks such as personal ships, Veil and Hollow arrive on their own dates, whatever the order. Only one Convergence is in Unseal or Confront at any time; if the world falls behind, the next may unseal as soon as the current one is released ([OPS-024](../11-production/ten-year-live-plan.md#42-what-is-pinned-whatever-the-order) in the [Ten-Year Live Plan](../11-production/ten-year-live-plan.md)). Earliest Unseal dates are published one Age ahead ([D-018](../../DECISIONS.md#d-018)).

**Ysolde is always last of the six,** because her release reveals Zero's name and the way to Hollow's heart. She becomes ready once four Celestials are freed and unseals only after the fifth ([D-016](../../DECISIONS.md#d-016)). Hollow itself opens for exploration by date at the eighth Long Night, whatever the state of the world. Convergence VII needs all six freed and unseals no earlier than 8.5 years after launch.

## 4. The Returner's Path

Each player's authored personal story ([Quest Architecture](quest-architecture.md#2-the-returners-path-personal-main-story)) unlocks one chapter per Age, and chapter 9 opens at the ninth Long Night. Chapters always run in order, so a player who joins in Year 6 starts at chapter 1 while the world around them is in Year 6. Chapters 3 to 6 each echo the Celestial freed in their Age, so their order follows the community's choices.

| Chapter | Theme | Personal beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Arrival | Identity | Your Arrival Dream stirs. A Lowhaven fisherman says you remind him of someone. |
| 2 · The Carrier | Belonging | You carry someone who can't walk, an echo of Orun |
| 3 · The Song | Grief | A mentor's loss. You choose what to remember. |
| 4 · The Courier | Courage | You deliver something no one else would |
| 5 · The Lantern | Sacrifice | You give up something you earned for someone else |
| 6 · The Workshop | Making | You build something for others |
| 7 · The Hour | Memory | In Veil you meet a memory of yourself that you don't remember living |
| 8 · The Net-Mender | Temptation | The Quiet offer you survival. A Lowhaven cottage. A choice. |
| 9 · The Oath | Resolve | You decide what you will swear |

For late joiners, each chapter's framing adapts to the world's current state [H2]. Chapter 2 in Year 6, for example, refers to Orun as already freed, through Chronicles.

## 5. Late joiners and Chronicles

A live Convergence is missed if you weren't there ([D-030](../../DECISIONS.md#d-030)). The Grand Library's Chronicles let anyone relive a past Convergence as a memory, with the real participants' names inscribed. Chronicles give lore, understanding and a cosmetic memento, never canonical titles, world-first honours or power.

- **NAR-001 [H1]** A player joining at any point can understand the current Age's stakes within 30 minutes, through a Herald briefing, Chronicles and what NPCs say.
- **NAR-002 [H2]** Catch-up for late joiners is narrative only: Chronicles, Herald briefings and adapted chapter framing. No catch-up grants power or canonical rewards. The world as a whole is helped only by the published Library-insight rule, which eases unfinished Discover and Prepare targets ([Celestial Convergence](../05-systems/celestial-convergence.md)).

## 6. The endings

### Success: the Dawn

- **Condition:** all seven Convergences are resolved before the tenth Long Night ends, and at least one Returner swears the Returners' Oath during it, which forges the Dawnkey.
- **The Dawn Rite:** during the 24-hour tenth Long Night, any Returner who chooses to can stand at any Wayshrine and swear the Returners' Oath. Its words are revealed only that night. Its meaning is *"We will carry each other."* Swearing is optional and can happen at any time in the 24 hours, so every time zone can take part.
- **The final hour:** one moment for the whole world. Solace breathes, and its fire turns outward into a road beyond the Wanderers.
- **After:** the world endures and the cycle is broken. **Oasis 2.0** [H3] begins on a second world circling a cold blue star ([The Story of the Oasis](../../STORY.md#9-the-endings-in-truth)).

### Failure: the Burning

- **Condition:** any Convergence is unresolved, or no Returner has sworn the Oath, when the tenth Long Night ends.
- **The final night:** a world-wide farewell. The Library opens to everyone, NPCs say goodbye, and Returners gather wherever they choose.
- **The final hour:** Solace breathes and the Oasis burns to glass. The live service ends as promised, ten years after launch ([D-011](../../DECISIONS.md#d-011)).
- **The coda:** the Library remembers. A shoreline, a fishing town, a child mending nets, and a clock reading **Year Zero**. The cycle turns, carrying the memory of every Returner.
- **After:** Archive Mode is released within seven days ([D-047](../../DECISIONS.md#d-047)).

### Promises that hold for both endings

- **NAR-010 [H1]** Global progress towards the Dawnkey is always visible on the World Clock and the Convergence boards.
- **NAR-011 [H1]** Difficulty scaling rules are published. Nothing is secretly adjusted towards either outcome.
- **NAR-012 [H1]** No individual or group can sabotage the world's progress.
- **NAR-013 [H2]** Both endings are fully produced in advance ([Ten-Year Live Plan](../11-production/ten-year-live-plan.md)).
- **NAR-014 [H3]** On the success path, every Returner who didn't swear at the Dawn Rite, including players who arrive afterwards, gets a personal Oath scene when they first enter Oasis 2.0.

## 7. Inspirations

| Source | What we take | What we change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Destiny*'s ten-year plan [R15] | A warning: a detailed story plan for a decade breaks on contact with live operations | We fix only the points in [Shape of the decade](#1-shape-of-the-decade) and keep everything else flexible |
| *Final Fantasy XIV: Endwalker* [R16] | A long saga can end while the world carries on | Our ending is announced on day one, and the world carries on only if the players earn it |
| *Game of Thrones* [W19] | A long-foreshadowed threat while factions fight; a rushed ending that soured years of story | Our threat is public from day one, and both endings are produced in full long before the tenth Long Night |
| *Foundation* [W26] | A known fall and a plan to soften it | The Grand Library keeps every story, and Archive Mode keeps it if the Oasis falls |

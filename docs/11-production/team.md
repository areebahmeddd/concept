# Team

Who builds and runs the game: the leadership roles, the departments, and roughly how many people each needs as the project moves from First Playable to launch and through the decade.

> **Version:** v0.1 · **Updated:** 10-10-2026 · **Owner:** Executive producer
>
> **Related:** [Scope and Horizons](scope-and-horizons.md) · [Ten-Year Live Plan](ten-year-live-plan.md) · [Risk Register](risk-register.md)

## 1. Leadership

| Role | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Executive producer | Budget, schedule, partners and the whole team |
| Game director | The game as a whole and every trade-off between systems |
| Creative director | Vision, pillars and tone |
| Technical director | Engine, servers, platforms and technical risk |
| Narrative director | Story, canon and the reveal graph |
| Art director | Painted Realism and every visual |
| Audio director | Music, sound and voices |
| AI director | The Loom, NPC Minds and the Weaver |
| Live producer | The ten-year calendar and live operations |
| Head of player safety | Moderation, age assurance and safety policy |
| Legal lead | Compliance, IP, contracts and counsel |

## 3. External partners

Work that scales up and down is done with partners, managed by the department that owns it.

| Partner work | Managed by | When it peaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art production and animation | Art | The last two years before launch, then each Age drop |
| Performance capture and voice recording | Audio and Narrative | Before launch, then each Age drop |
| Large-scale testing | Quality assurance | Betas, launch and each Age drop |
| Localisation | Narrative | Before launch, then continuously |
| Moderation and player support | Player safety and support | From launch, scaling with players |
| Cultural consultants | Narrative | Before each culture enters production |

## 4. Shape over time

- **First Playable (about two years).** A small senior team proves the unique bets: NPC Minds, the Weaver and the feel of combat and traversal. Hiring waits for the H0 exit gate ([Scope and Horizons](scope-and-horizons.md)).
- **Production to launch (about five years).** The team grows to its peak, and partners carry most content volume in the final two years.
- **The live decade.** The team settles to a steady size that ships one Age drop a year and quarterly updates without crunch ([Ten-Year Live Plan](ten-year-live-plan.md)).

## 2. Departments and headcount

Numbers are in-house staff at each stage. They are planning targets, set again at each milestone.

The studio is small on purpose: remote-first, with one small hub, hiring wherever the best people live, and using partners for work that peaks and falls. Small teams have shipped award-winning and hugely popular games: *Clair Obscur* with about 30 core staff [G21], *Palworld* with about 55 [G22], and *Albion Online*, a cross-platform MMO, with about 30 at launch and around 120 today [G23].

## 4. Shape over time

- **First Playable (about two years).** A small senior team proves the unique bets: NPC Minds, the Weaver and the feel of combat and traversal. Hiring waits for the H0 exit gate ([Scope and Horizons](scope-and-horizons.md)).
- **Production to launch (about five years).** The team grows to its peak, and partners carry most content volume in the final two years.
- **The live decade.** The team settles to a steady size that ships one Age drop a year and quarterly updates without crunch ([Ten-Year Live Plan](ten-year-live-plan.md)).

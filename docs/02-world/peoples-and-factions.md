## 1. Cultural care rules

Every tradition we draw on is researched with care. We separate documented tradition from later retellings, and we work with consultants. These rules are binding.

| ID | Rule | Precedent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WLD-050 [H1]** | Every culture has a **Culture Sheet** recording: its invented identity, the real traditions that inspire it (named), what is documented and what is adapted, the consultants engaged, sacred or off-limits elements, and review status | None |
| **WLD-051 [H1]** | Cultures are invented peoples inspired by traditions, never depictions of real peoples. No real religion is shown as a fantasy faction. | None |
| **WLD-052 [H1]** | Sacred elements are never loot or destructible scenery, and are never combined across traditions without consultant sign-off | Criticism of shrine destruction in *Assassin's Creed Shadows* [L25] |
| **WLD-053 [H1]** | Belief is shown from the inside: each culture's view makes sense on its own terms | *The Northman* [W1] |
| **WLD-054 [H1]** | Where a real tradition is closely evoked, consultants from that tradition review it, and co-creation is preferred | *Never Alone* [L24], *Ghost of Tsushima* [L23] |
| **WLD-055 [H1]** | A culture's sheet is approved by its consultants before any of that culture's content enters production | None |

## 3. Factions

Factions want different things from the countdown, and that conflict is where many quests come from.

| Faction | Goal | Stance on the Celestials | Relationship with players | Horizon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Keepers of the Grand Library** | Preserve all memory and decode the records of the Seven Days | Believe the six can be freed | Mentors and quest-givers, led by the Herald | H1 |
| **The Watch** (one per jurisdiction, such as the Halcyon Watch) | Keep Civic Law | Practical | Law enforcement, bounty boards and the Watch posts where fines are paid ([Law, Crime and Bounties](../05-systems/law-crime-and-bounties.md)) | H1 |
| **The Artisan Guilds** | Craft, trade and reputation | Neutral; they profit from the preparations | Artisans and commissions ([Economy and Crafting](../05-systems/economy-and-crafting.md)) | H1 |
| **The Engine Houses** (Iron Meridian) | Industrial power. Some want to profit from the end of the world. | Some see the Conjunction as a market | Employers and rivals | H1 |
| **The Skyferry Company** | Keep its monopoly on moon transport | Neutral | Transport, jobs and smuggling plots | H1 |
| **The Quiet** | A secret cult that welcomes the Burning as a cleansing and a remaking | Worship the False Celestial without knowing who he is | Antagonists. Players can infiltrate it ([WLD-056](#3-factions)). | H1 |
| **The Lumenari Stillness** | Uphold the law of non-interference | Watch, don't act | Gatekeepers on Lumen | H1 |
| **The Lumenari Intercessors** | Break the Stillness and help the Oasis | Act | Allies. How many Returners side with them changes how the Lumenari appear at the Dawn Rite. It never changes the outcome. | H1–H2 |
| **The Returners' Concord** | The loose fellowship of every Returner | Free the six | The shared identity behind each player's contribution. It is not a clan ([Social, Voice and Clans](../07-multiplayer/social-voice-and-clans.md)). | H1 |

- **WLD-056 [H1]** A player can join the Quiet through an authored infiltration storyline. Nothing a player does as a member lowers global progress or helps the Burning.

**Faction reputation** runs from −100 (Hated) to +100 (Exalted), separate from levels and ranks. It opens faction quests, vendors and titles, and never grants combat power.

## 4. Naming rules (light conlang)

Each culture has naming rules, so authored and generated names stay consistent. The AI follows the same rules ([NPC Minds and Conversation](../06-ai/npc-minds-and-conversation.md)).

| Culture | Sound | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hearthfolk | Plain English compounds | Millbrook, Hearthwick, Tam Harrow |
| Cairnfolk | Short, with hard consonants | Highcairn, Brannoc, Tesk |
| Pelagians | Liquid and rich in vowels | Pelagia, Aulora, Merren |
| Caravan Kingdoms | Rhythmic, with honorific titles | Qasr Amber, Sahr, Idrin al-Lume |
| Meridians | Clipped, technical nicknames | Vantor, Rivet, Neon Steps |
| Northfolk | Long and saga-like | Hallowmere, Ysolde, Kingsrest |
| Lumenari | Soft sibilants | Seren, Ilsa Tellmar |

## 5. Inspirations

| Source | What we take | What we change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IOI in *Ready Player One* [R1] | A corporation that would sell salvation | The Engine Houses are one faction among many, divided inside, with members players can win over |
| Piltover and Zaun in *Arcane* [W14] | A city split by class, where invention belongs to the few | The Iron Meridian's Engine Houses sell what Vantor gave away, and the Undercity's tinkerers keep his free designs alive |
| The Prime Directive in *Star Trek* [W5] | A law forbidding interference in another world's fate | The law has cost the Lumenari an age of guilt and split them in two, and players take sides |
| The ETO in *The Three-Body Problem* [W18] | A secret group that welcomes the end of the world | The Quiet worship the coming Burning without knowing Zero, and players can infiltrate them but never help the Burning |
| Tolkien's invented languages [W6] | Names that sound like they come from one culture | Naming rules only, not full languages, so writers and the AI can follow them |

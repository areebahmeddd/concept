## 1. World layout

The rough map below shows where each region lies.

```text
                          THE FROSTMARCH
                    (polar ice, aurora, old kingdoms)
                                 |
  THE DROWNED REACH  ---  THE HEARTHLANDS  ---  THE IRON MERIDIAN
  (western ocean:         Halcyon, Lowhaven,     (eastern coastal
   Pelagia, Murmur,       Sennight ruins)         megacity)
   Saltglass)                    |           THE VERDANT SPINE
                                 |           (north-eastern mountains
                                 |            and forests)
                         THE EMBER WASTES
                       (southern glass deserts)

  THE STORMCROWN floats above the southern sea, between the Ember Wastes
  and the Drowned Reach, reached by sky-reef or by flight.
```

**Why the land looks like this.** Westerly winds carry ocean rain over the Hearthlands, and the Verdant Spine catches it, which grows its dense forests. A rain shadow lies beyond the Spine. The southern deserts sit under a permanent belt of high pressure, made worse by the ancient Burning. The Stormcrown's isles float on lift from Kestra's bound power, above a warm sea that feeds endless storms. The Frostmarch is polar. The Iron Meridian grew where seams of coal-like *sparkstone* meet a deep harbour. The places are invented, and the climate follows real principles.

## 2. Distinctness standard (VIS-007)

Every Domain must be distinct on five axes, reviewed at each milestone:

| Axis | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Natural Law | Does it change how you play here? ([World Laws](world-laws.md#4-natural-law-by-domain)) |
| Palette and form | Can you recognise it from one screenshot? ([Art and Audio Direction](../01-vision/art-and-audio-direction.md#2-domain-palettes)) |
| Music and sound | Can you recognise it with your eyes closed? |
| Traversal | Does moving through it feel different? |
| Culture | Do its people live, believe and speak differently? ([Peoples and Factions](peoples-and-factions.md)) |

Each region also serves at least one primary player motivation that no other region leads with, using the Quantic Foundry model [R27].

## 6. The Lawless Reaches

The Lawless Reaches are places where no jurisdiction holds. Civic Law and the Watch don't reach them, and PvP is open ([D-025](../../DECISIONS.md#d-025), [PvP and Protection](../05-systems/pvp-and-protection.md)). Natural Law still applies. Each Reach has an in-world reason for being lawless, and the HUD always shows a clear warning at its border.

| Reach | Region | Why no one rules it | What draws people |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Sennight Marches** | Hearthlands, around the ruined city | Haunted by the Seven Days, so no ruler will claim it | Relics of the battle and its Long Night scenes |
| **The Glass Badlands** | Southern Ember Wastes | Too hot and too far for the Caravan Kingdoms | Rich glass-cores and Gravemaw nests |
| **The Undercity Depths** | Below the Iron Meridian | Abandoned by the Engine Houses and run by tinker gangs | Old blueprints and black markets |
| **The Outer Isles** | The Stormcrown's rim | Storm-torn isles beyond the sky-nomads' routes | Rare sky-flora and courier contraband |

- **WLD-045 [H1]** Every Lawless Reach is optional. No main-story or personal-saga objective requires entering one, and every resource found there can also be obtained lawfully.

## 7. Content density rules

- **WLD-040 [H1]** No stretch of travel lasts longer than about 2 rt minutes at normal speed without a point of interest: a landmark, encounter, resource, NPC, Egg or view.
- **WLD-041 [H1]** Every region has at least three different kinds of route between its major cities, such as road, cliff path, river and sky.
- **WLD-042 [H1]** Wayshrines are spaced so that, once discovered, one is within about 5 rt minutes of any point.
- **WLD-043 [H1]** Fast travel between Wayshrines exists, but each Wayshrine must first be reached on foot or by flight.

The sources behind our central inspiration, Ernest Cline's *Ready Player* books and film, along with precedents for ending a game world and research on why people play together for years.

## Ready Player One and Two: what inspires us, and how we differ

- **[R1]** *Ready Player One* (Ernest Cline, Crown, 16 August 2011) · S
  - *Fact:* James Halliday's will hides an Easter egg in the OASIS. Whoever finds it inherits his fortune and control of the OASIS. The path runs through three keys (Copper, Jade, Crystal) and three gates. A public **Scoreboard** made the first finder famous overnight. The corporate antagonist IOI wanted to paywall the OASIS, fill it with ads and end anonymity. The OASIS has 27 sectors whose zones flag magic, technology and PvP; the school planet Ludus is PvP-free.
  - → **Lesson:**
    - We take a world-scale quest with world-scale stakes, a public record of deeds (the Chronicle Board), zones with rules, and the joy of obsessive puzzle hunting (Eggs).
    - We change the stake to collective survival, not one winner taking everything, and give zone rules a physical cause inside the world (Natural Law) instead of admin flags.
    - We design against IOI: no paid power, no ads, privacy by default.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready_Player_One> · <https://www.litcharts.com/lit/ready-player-one/summary> · <https://www.shmoop.com/study-guides/ready-player-one/summary.html>
- **[R4]** *Ready Player Two* (Cline, 2020) · S
  - *Fact:* the ONI neural headset (full sensory recording and playback, with a 12-hour safety limit). Halliday's **"Seven Shards of the Siren's Soul"** quest. A rogue Anorak AI traps about 500 million users. Reviews were largely negative (for example Den of Geek's "soulless sequel" and IGN's 4/10).
  - → **Lesson:** "seven pieces gathered across worlds" already exists, so our seven must feel personal and tragic, not like a fetch quest; spectacle and references can't replace heart. We change the pieces into people: each Celestial has a life and an Oath. We change the villain too: a rogue AI ruling the world is close to the False Celestial, so he is human greed and fear, never "the AI did it" ([Celestials](../docs/03-narrative/celestials.md)).
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready_Player_Two> · <https://www.denofgeek.com/books/ready-player-two-book-review-ernest-cline/> · <https://locusmag.com/2021/01/paul-di-filippo-reviews-ernest-clines-ready-player-two>
- **[R7]** *Ready Player One* film (Spielberg, Warner Bros., 29 March 2018) · S
  - *Fact:* about $607 million worldwide. The challenges were changed from the book (a car race; *The Shining* replacing *Blade Runner*; Atari's *Adventure* at the finale).
  - → **Lesson:** we take spectacle set pieces and a finale that pays off a shared history. Ours are the Long Nights and the final Convergence, experienced together by everyone at once.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready_Player_One_(film)>
- **[R8]** Cline on "Ready Player Zero"; see also **[L1]**, Tor.com (2020) · S
  - *Fact:* Cline said a prequel would more likely be called "Ready Player Zero". No book had been announced by October 2026.
  - → **Lesson:** our title is a deliberate working homage ([D-004](../DECISIONS.md#d-004)) with real collision risk. Inside our fiction, "Zero" points to the cycle's first Returner ([Celestials](../docs/03-narrative/celestials.md)).
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Cline> · <https://tor.com/2020/11/12/are-we-ready-for-a-ready-player-one-trilogy-ernie-cline-hints-at-a-prequel-book>
- **[R9]** Readyverse Studios (January 2024) and *OPEN* (announced March 2024) · S
  - *Fact:* a venture of Futureverse, Cline and Dan Farah that holds the *Ready Player One* rights from Warner Bros. *OPEN* is a multi-IP battle royale. Its 2025 to 2026 status is unverified.
  - → **Lesson:** the IP is actively licensed, so the legal risk is real ([Legal and Compliance](../docs/12-legal/compliance.md#1-intellectual-property-and-permitted-homages)).
  - <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/futureverse-and-ready-player-one-creator-and-producer-team-up-to-launch-readyverse-studios-bringing-leading-ip-and-brands-to-the-metaverse-302025939.html> · <https://www.forbes.com/sites/dbloom/2024/03/11/ernest-clines-readyverse-studios-launches-open-trailer-its-first-metaverse/>

## Ending a game world: law, precedent and player trust

- **[R11]** *The Crew* shutdown and "Stop Killing Games" · S
  - *Fact:* Ubisoft shut down *The Crew* on 31 March 2024 and revoked licences, with no offline mode. Ross Scott launched Stop Killing Games on 2 April 2024.
  - → **Lesson:** players resent losing access without warning and with nothing to keep. We take the warning seriously: our end is announced on day one, and Archive Mode gives players something to keep ([D-047](../DECISIONS.md#d-047)).
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crew_(video_game)>
- **[R12]** EU Citizens' Initiative "Stop Destroying Videogames" · P
  - *Fact:* 1,294,188 verified signatures. On **16 June 2026** the Commission replied that it "cannot propose a legal obligation to keep video games playable after they stop being provided commercially". It will instead convene a voluntary end-of-life code of conduct by the end of 2026, and the campaign is targeting the Digital Fairness Act.
  - → **Lesson:** we announce our planned ten-year end from day one, and Archive Mode ([D-047](../DECISIONS.md#d-047)) goes further than today's law, in the direction regulation is moving.
  - <https://citizens-initiative.europa.eu/news/european-commission-replies-stop-destroying-videogames-initiative-2026-06-16_en> · <https://heise.de/-11154579>
- **[R14]** California AB 2426 (in force from 1 January 2025) · S
  - *Fact:* sellers of digital goods must disclose that the buyer gets a revocable licence, not ownership, unless certain conditions apply (subscriptions and permanently downloadable offline goods are exempt).
  - → **Lesson:** cosmetic purchases carry licence disclosures, and Archive Mode strengthens our position ([Legal and Compliance](../docs/12-legal/compliance.md#6-consumer-law-and-the-planned-ten-year-end)).
  - <https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2024/12/ab-2426-new-california-law-requires-clear-licensing-disclosures-for-digital-goods>
- **[R15]** Destiny's "ten-year" plan · S
  - *Fact:* a 2010 Activision and Bungie contract planned Destiny releases through 2020. *The Final Shape* (2024) concluded the saga, and the game continued afterwards. In 2015 Bungie called a pre-written ten-year story "comical".
  - → **Lesson:** we take the need for a decade-long arc. We change how it is held: the fixed points (the Conjunction, the seven Celestials, the two endings) are canon, and the path between them stays live ([Ten-Year Live Plan](../docs/11-production/ten-year-live-plan.md)).
  - <https://www.engadget.com/2012-05-21-activision-bungie-contract-reveals-four-game-plan-code-named-de.html>
- **[R16]** *FFXIV: Endwalker* (2021) · S
  - *Fact:* marketed as the finale of a roughly ten-year saga (2010 to 2021). The game then continued with a new arc.
  - → **Lesson:** a saga can end and the world go on. That is our success path into Oasis 2.0.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIV:_Endwalker>
- **[R17] / [R18]** *Dragalia Lost* (ended 30 November 2022) and *NieR Re[in]carnation* (final chapter 28 March 2024, servers closed 29 April 2024) · S
  - *Fact:* both finished their story and then closed, with no offline version.
  - → **Lesson:** a story-timed end is accepted when it is honest. What players resent is the missing offline version, which is what Archive Mode fixes.
  - <https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2022/11/psa-dragalia-lost-has-ended-its-service-as-planned> · <https://gameinformer.com/2024/01/24/nier-reincarnation-shuts-down-in-april>
- **[R19] / [R20]** *Knockout City* (private server edition, 2023) and *Mega Man X DiVE Offline* (2023) · S
  - *Fact:* both shipped player-hosted or offline editions when the live service ended.
  - → **Lesson:** Archive Mode is feasible and has precedent.
  - <https://gematsu.com/2023/02/knockout-city-to-end-service-on-june-6-private-hosted-server-edition-to-be-released-for-pc> · <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mega_Man_X_Dive>

## Collective goals with real stakes

- **[R21]** *FFXIV* Ishgard Restoration (from patch 5.11, 2019) · P
  - *Fact:* collective crafting and gathering on each world filled a progress gauge that rebuilt a city in stages.
  - → **Lesson:** we take the Prepare phase of a Convergence: crafters and gatherers matter to the endgame, not just fighters. We change the effect: Prepare gauges reshape the ground of the coming fight (bridges, root-roads).
  - <https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/bastion-uk/pressreleases/unite-to-restore-the-holy-see-of-ishgard-today-in-final-fantasy-xiv-shadowbringers-patch-5-dot-11-2942591>
- **[R22]** *EVE Online* Triglavian Invasion (2019 to 2020) · P
  - *Fact:* a year-long arc whose final chapter asked players to choose sides, with lasting consequences for the map (the region Pochven).
  - → **Lesson:** player choices can permanently reshape the world. Our global outcomes work this way ([D-030](../DECISIONS.md#d-030)), in one world for everyone.
  - <https://pressreleases.triplepointpr.com/2020/05/20/ccp-games-commence-the-final-chapter-of-the-year-long-invasion-expansion-for-eve-online/>
- **[R23]** *Pokémon GO* Global Challenge (2019) · P
  - *Fact:* players worldwide completed a three-part challenge that unlocked bonuses for everyone.
  - → **Lesson:** global contributions that any activity can feed let solo and casual players feel part of the whole ([D-024](../DECISIONS.md#d-024)).
  - <https://pokemongo.com/en/news/globalchallenge-ultrabonus-jirachi>
- **[R24]** *Fortnite* "The End" (2019) · S
  - *Fact:* more than 7 million live viewers (reported by Epic).
  - → **Lesson:** people watch world-ending events as culture, so the Conjunction is also designed as a broadcast spectacle ([Recording and Replays](../docs/05-systems/recording-and-replays.md)).
  - <https://www.pocketgamer.biz/fortnites-the-end-event-was-viewed-by-seven-million-people-live>

## Why people play together for years

- **[R25]** Richard Bartle, "Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, Spades" (1996) · P
  - *Fact:* four player types: Achievers, Explorers, Socialisers, Killers.
  - → **Lesson:** our loops serve all four: ranks and Mastery Trials for Achievers, Eggs and moons for Explorers, Wards and clans for Socialisers, and opt-in PvP and bounties for Killers ([Core Loops](../docs/04-gameplay/core-loops.md)).
  - <https://mud.co.uk/richard/hcds.htm>
- **[R26]** Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski (2006), *The Motivational Pull of Video Games* · P
  - *Fact:* autonomy, competence and relatedness predict enjoyment and continued play. Relatedness was significant in multiplayer.
  - → **Lesson:** the theory behind our pillars: autonomy (an open world and self-chosen Oaths), competence (mastery-based levels, never paid power) and relatedness (a shared fate and the Returners' Oath).
  - <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8>
- **[R27]** Nick Yee and Quantic Foundry, Gamer Motivation Model · P
  - *Fact:* 12 motivations, from more than 400,000 (now more than 2 million) respondents.
  - → **Lesson:** we use it to check that each Domain and each activity serves a distinct motivation, so no region is interchangeable.
  - <https://quanticfoundry.com/> · <https://gdcvault.com/play/1025742/A-Deep-Dive-into-the>

## Real-time clocks

- **[R28]** *Animal Crossing* runs on the real clock, and changing the system clock ("time travel") is tolerated · S → **Lesson:** a real-time anchor creates daily ritual. We anchor only the Long Year and the Long Night to real time; the Oasis day drifts ([D-013](../DECISIONS.md#d-013)). <https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Time_travel>
- **[R29]** *Pokémon GO* day and night follow local time · S → **Lesson:** anchoring to local time disadvantages some regions, so a drifting global day is fairer. <https://www.pocketgamer.com/pokemon-go/nightime-spawns>
- **M27** FFXIV: one Eorzean day lasts 70 real minutes ([Industry and Live Service](industry-and-live-service.md)).

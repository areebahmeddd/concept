# References: Anime and Manga

Verified facts about the anime and manga that shape our abilities, antagonists, tone and machines, and what we take from each without copying.

## Ability systems: the roots of Arts and Oaths

- **[J1]** *Naruto* (Masashi Kishimoto, 1999 to 2014) · S (fan wiki)
  - *Fact:* chakra takes one of five natures (Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Water). Jutsu use twelve basic hand seals named after the zodiac animals. Fans document a neat weakness cycle, but the manga is inconsistent about it.
  - → **Lesson:** we take elemental identity with learnable techniques, and hand seals as the spirit of Signature Art inputs (CMB-035). We change the structure: our six Attunements (Root, Tide, Storm, Ember, Spark, Hour) form one counter cycle as a game rule, and an Attunement is a leaning, never a class lock.
  - <https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_Transformation>
- **[J2]** *Jujutsu Kaisen* (Gege Akutami, 2018 to 2024) · S
  - *Fact:* Cursed Techniques are innate. Domain Expansion creates a space where a technique is boosted and guaranteed to hit. **Binding Vows** trade restriction for power, with backlash if broken. Akutami acknowledges Hunter x Hunter's influence.
  - → **Lesson:** we take Binding Vows into Oaths ([D-026](../DECISIONS.md#d-026)) and space-claiming ultimates into Domain Arts [H2]. We change the ultimates: Domain Arts are short, readable, and obey each Domain's Natural Law instead of guaranteeing a hit.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujutsu_Kaisen> · <https://twinfinite.net/guides/what-was-itadori-sukunas-binding-vow-in-jujutsu-kaisen-explained/>
- **[J3]** *Hunter x Hunter* (Yoshihiro Togashi, 1998 onward) · S
  - *Fact:* six Nen types (Enhancer, Emitter, Manipulator, Transmuter, Conjurer, Specialist). Under **Vows and Limitations**, "the stricter the limits, the stronger the ability". Kurapika's Chain Jail works only on the Phantom Troupe, and using it on anyone else kills him.
  - → **Lesson:** we take the cleanest precedent for Oaths: self-chosen limits make personal builds that resist copying the meta. We change their reach: Oaths are also the story's spine, because the Celestials ascended by keeping Oaths and the False Celestial broke his.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_%C3%97_Hunter>
- **[J4]** *Solo Leveling* (Chugong web novel 2016; webtoon 2018 to 2021; A-1 Pictures anime 2024) · S
  - *Fact:* Sung Jinwoo is chosen by "the System" as its only player and can level without limit. *Solo Leveling: Arise* (Netmarble) launched on 8 May 2024 after more than 12 million pre-registrations. (Hunter ranks E to S and dungeon breaks come from general knowledge; the detail is unverified.)
  - → **Lesson:** we take the pleasure of visible, earned growth, and gates that break into the world as a model for Long Night surges. We change the ceiling: levels stop at 10 ([D-021](../DECISIONS.md#d-021)), and the sense of limitless growth comes from mastery and Oaths instead.
  - <https://animecorner.me/solo-leveling-anime-by-a-1-pictures-officially-announced-teaser-visual-and-trailer-revealed/> · <https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20240417ae90035>

## Antagonists, tragedy and theme

- **[J7]** *Dororo* (Osamu Tezuka, 1967; MAPPA and Tezuka Productions anime, 2019) · S
  - *Fact:* a father trades his newborn son's body parts to 48 demons for power, and Hyakkimaru wins each part back by defeating its demon.
  - → **Lesson:** we take the structure of reclaiming stolen power piece by piece. We change who gets it back: each freed Celestial returns its Oath to every Returner and to the Dawnkey, not to one hero.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dororo_(2019_TV_series)>
- **[J9]** *Vinland Saga* (Makoto Yukimura; Wit Studio 2019, MAPPA 2023) · S
  - *Fact:* Thorfinn moves from revenge to pacifism ("You have no enemies"). Yukimura refuses to turn back towards violence for fans. The anime is rated TV-MA in the US.
  - → **Lesson:** we take transformation over power: Maris's Convergence and the understanding routes that decide Zero's fate ([D-017](../DECISIONS.md#d-017)). We change the depiction to stay inside the T rating ([D-006](../DECISIONS.md#d-006)).
  - <https://www.espinof.com/anime/te-molesta-que-thorfinn-no-sea-cafre-creador-vinland-saga-deja-claro-que-su-manga-no-para-ti-quieres-violencia-lee-shingeki-no-kyojin> · <https://m.imdb.com/title/tt10529444/parentalguide>
- **[J10]** *Berserk* (Kentaro Miura, 1989 onward) · S
  - *Fact:* after Miura's death (May 2021), the series continued from June 2022 under Studio Gaga and Kouji Mori. Griffith sacrificing his own band for ambition is the genre's archetype of a friend who betrays everything (a common critical reading).
  - → **Lesson:** we take the emotional core of the False Celestial: a trusted friend, not a born monster. We change his motive and his end: he acts from fear, the community decides his fate ([D-017](../DECISIONS.md#d-017)), and the depiction stays restrained.
  - <https://animecorner.me/berserk-manga-to-continue-on-june-24-under-koji-moris-supervision/>
- **N19** *Fate/stay night* and *Fate/Zero* (Type-Moon): seven Masters and seven Servants in seven classes. → We take seven ideologies in conflict. We change the shape: one betrayal and six victims, with none of its class taxonomy or terms.
- **N20** *Demon Slayer*: the Twelve Kizuki (six Upper, six Lower) under Muzan. → We take ranked antagonists with tragic backstories. We change their nature: the six Celestials are bound, not evil, which avoids the cliché.
- **N21** *Frieren* (Yamada and Abe; Madhouse, 2023): time and memory, and a meteor shower that recurs every 50 years. → We take the feeling of a rare recurring night and of long-lived people watching time pass, for the Long Night and for NPCs who age across the decade.
- **N22** *Mushishi* (Yuki Urushibara): quiet, episodic folk mysteries. → We take its tone for small, quiet micro-events.
- **N30** *Berserk* Golden Age film III: initially rated R18+ in Japan. → A tone ceiling we never cross.

- **[J11]** *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood* (Bones, 2009 to 2010; manga by Hiromu Arakawa) · S
  - *Fact:* alchemy obeys Equivalent Exchange: "to obtain, something of equal value must be lost". The Philosopher's Stone is made by trapping thousands of human souls, and the antagonist, Father, uses such power to try to become a god.
  - → **Lesson:** power feels earned when it has a visible price, and the worst power is taken from other people. We take both: every Oath trades a constraint for its boon, and Zero's strength is six Oaths stolen from friends.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fullmetal_Alchemist> · <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fullmetal_Alchemist:_Brotherhood>
- **[J12]** *One Piece* (Eiichiro Oda, 1997 onward) · S
  - *Fact:* a stretch of history has been erased from all government-approved sources, and uncovering it drives the long mystery. Devil Fruit powers come with a weakness: users can't swim. It holds the Guinness record for the most copies published for one comic series by a single author.
  - → **Lesson:** an erased history is a mystery players can chase for years. We take it for the Library records Zero erased, which Returners piece back together through Eggs and Chronicles.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Piece> · <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Robin>
- **[J13]** *Pluto* (Naoki Urasawa, 2003 to 2009; Netflix anime 2023) · S
  - *Fact:* a retelling of an *Astro Boy* arc, led by Gesicht, a robot detective. Its robots feel love, hate, hope and despair.
  - → **Lesson:** machines become characters when their feelings have consequences. We take it for companion Automata and the Wardens, whose loyalty and grief come from what they have lived through.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluto_(manga)>

## Mechs and giant monsters

- **[J5]** *Mobile Suit Gundam* (Sunrise and Tomino, 1979) · S
  - *Fact:* credited with founding the "Real Robot" genre, with mechs as mass-produced weapons. **Minovsky particles** jam radar and guidance, which justifies close-range mech combat. Tomino (second-hand): "The reason Gundam still works is the Minovsky particle."
  - → **Lesson:** we take the rule that every fantastic machine needs a physical reason for how it fights. Our reason is Natural Law: each Domain's resonance shapes range, guidance and flight ([World Laws](../docs/02-world/world-laws.md)). We change the setting around them: Frames are manufactured, maintained, insured and regulated rather than military super-weapons, and none of Gundam's terms are used.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Suit_Gundam> · <https://www.gundam-the-origin.net/en/world/orientation25.html>
- **[J6]** *Pacific Rim* (Guillermo del Toro, 2013; Legendary and Warner Bros.) · S
  - *Fact:* Jaegers are too much for one pilot, so two pilots share "the Drift". Del Toro avoided forced 3D depth so the robots wouldn't feel "human scale". Kaiju are classed in categories I to V. (No del Toro quote on mech weight was verified; don't attribute one.)
  - → **Lesson:** we take multi-crew roles (pilot, gunner, engineer) for Colossal Frames [H2], scale that is staged through camera, sound and ground shake, and colossi graded for readable escalation. We use none of its terms or designs ([Legal and Compliance](../docs/12-legal/compliance.md#1-intellectual-property-and-permitted-homages)).
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Rim_(film)>

## Action choreography and virtual worlds

- **[J8]** *Sword of the Stranger* (Studio Bones, 2007, directed by Masahiro Andō) · S
  - *Fact:* its closing duel, under five minutes long, is widely cited among the best animated fights. It is credited to action animation supervisor Yutaka Nakamura.
  - → **Lesson:** we take its duel choreography as the feel reference for combat: weight, spacing, and silence before the strike.
  - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sword_of_the_Stranger>
- **N26** *Shangri-La Frontier* (Katarina, web novel 2017) and *Sword Art Online* (Reki Kawahara): stories set inside virtual worlds. → We take the tension of a world that may be made. We change how it is handled: the nature of the Oasis stays ambiguous and is never revealed as "just a simulation" ([D-012](../DECISIONS.md#d-012)).
